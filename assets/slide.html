Android TV Ad-Block Setup Flow

Zero Hardware. Zero Config. 1-Click Deployment.

Get ad-free streaming directly on your smart TV using your laptop in under 20 seconds.

Step 1 TV Setup

Developer Mode

Go to Settings > About, tap Build 7 times to enable Developer Mode. Turn USB Debugging ON, and note your TV IP.

Step 2 Computer 1-Click

Double-Click Setup

Connect laptop & TV to same Wi-Fi. Run setup-windows.bat (Win) or setup-mac.command (Mac).

Step 3 Remote Approval

Accept TV Prompts

Watch the TV and press OK on 2 prompts: Allow USB Debugging (always) & VPN Connection Request.

Step 4 ✔ Ready

Enjoy Zero Ads!

Stream seamlessly. Zero ads on Prime & JioHotstar; Netflix whitelisted for flawless connection.
